# Gestures

> **Kind:** procedure · **Status:** current · **Stands on:** [keyboard-and-mouse-guide.md](keyboard-and-mouse-guide.md)

What the mouse and the keyboard let you do beyond a single key or a single
click: a double click, a sequence of keys, a rest of the pointer, and a right
click. F1 and Ctrl+Shift+P are the two ways to see what else works where you
are.

## A click selects

A left click puts the selection where you click. Of the gestures in this guide,
only the left click moves the selection.

## A double click opens

In the Files pane, a double click on a file opens it. Where a part of the
program answers a double click with more than a single click does, F1 names
it, because every key and every click that works where you are is on that
list.

## A sequence of keys

The program can tell a sequence of two key presses, done one after another,
from two separate presses, and treat the sequence as one gesture, a chord. No
part of the program binds a chord to anything yet, so no key does more as the
first step of one today.

## Rest the pointer

Stop the pointer over a part and wait about half a second. A small window opens
and says what the part is. See [the tooltip](tooltip-guide.md) for the window,
the keys that show more of it, and what closes it.

## A right click opens a menu

A right click opens the menu of the part under the pointer, at the pointer, and
does not move the selection. See [the context menu](context-menu-guide.md) for
the window, the keys that add more menus, and the command that opens the same
menu with no pointer.

## Find the rest

1. Press F1. A window lists every key, click, double click, chord and rest of
   the pointer that does something where the selection is now, with what each
   one does.
2. Press Ctrl+Shift+P. Type a few letters of what you want to do, and press
   Enter to run it. This finds a gesture that has no key or click of its own,
   such as a menu or a tooltip run from a part with no pointer on it.

See [the keyboard and the mouse](keyboard-and-mouse-guide.md) for the full list
of keys, and [the pointer](pointer-guide.md) for what lights under it.
